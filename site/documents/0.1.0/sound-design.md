## Plan

One continuous instrumental bed carries the episode from a gentle evening theme through hesitation, playful reassurance, and a quiet close. Rain enters with the living room and remains beneath the music through the final frame; two softly rounded thunder rolls retain comparable weight. Close, restrained fabric sounds and two small musical accents support the physical comedy without turning the storm into a character or making the soundtrack startling.
